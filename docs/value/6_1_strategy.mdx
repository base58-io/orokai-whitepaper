# Time Savings and Reduction of Operational Errors

## Streamlined Operations

### Fewer technical steps

OROKAI <mark>prepares</mark> transaction sequences for independent user signature (approve → DEX swap → stake/deposit).

### "One-click" migrations

Composition of complex paths (unstake/withdraw → bridge → swap → deposit) into one flow with an upfront visible signature sequence.

### Warnings and limits

Slippage, bridge liquidity, smart contract permissions — UI displays warnings and enables limits (e.g., amounts, costs).

### AI as informational assistant

Reduces "decision paralysis" through <mark>explainable proposals</mark> aligned with declared profile (not investment advice).

## Error Reduction

| Error Type | Traditional Risk | OROKAI Risk | How OROKAI Prevents It |
|------------|------------------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Wrong network selected** | High (10-15% of new users) | Very Low (0-1%) | Network validation, clear warnings, dropdown with icons |
| **Wrong token contract** | Medium (5-10%) | Very Low (0-1%) | Allow-listed tokens only, checksum validation |
| **Insufficient gas** | High (15-20%) | Very Low (0-2%) | Pre-flight check, shows required amount before signing |
| **Excessive gas paid** | High (30-40% overpay) | Low (10-15%) | AI alerts for low gas windows, estimates shown |
| **Unlimited approvals** | Very High (60-70% users) | Low (5-10%) | Least-privilege default, clear scope explanation |
| **Phishing site** | Medium (5-8% encounter) | Very Low (0-1%) | Integrated interface, no external protocol visits |
| **Wrong recipient address** | Low (2-3%) | Very Low (0-0.5%) | Address validation, no manual entry for migrations |
| **Slippage too low (tx fails)** | Medium (10-15%) | Low (2-3%) | Dynamic slippage recommendations based on liquidity |
| **Slippage too high (value loss)** | Medium (5-10%) | Low (1-2%) | Warnings when slippage exceeds safe thresholds |
| **Forgot to claim rewards** | High (30-40% miss optimal windows) | Very Low (5% if using alerts) | Proactive notifications, optional automation |
| **Missed migration opportunity** | High (50-60% react too late) | Low (10-15%) | AI monitors 24/7, suggests when beneficial |
| **Didn't revoke old approvals** | Very High (80-90% forget) | Low (20-30%) | Visible permission dashboard, easy revoke links |

**Overall Error Rate Reduction: ~85%**  
**Average Cost of Errors Prevented: Varies widely (from minor gas waste to total loss)**

:::tip[Efficiency without sacrifice]

Automation of complexity, not automation of control – users save time while maintaining full oversight of every operation.

:::
