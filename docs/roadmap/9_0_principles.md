# Principles and Assumptions

## Core Development Principles

### Non-custodial & prepare-and-sign

OROKAI prepares operations; user signs them independently in their wallet.

### DEX-first

Crypto exchanges through external DEX (allow-list); no "best execution" claims.

### Compliance gates

Features requiring regulatory approval (CEX, KYC, RWA, securities) are unlocked only after meeting jurisdictional requirements and establishing agreements with licensed partners.

### No return guarantees

Rewards/returns are variable and not guaranteed; they originate from external protocol parameters or network activity.

:::warning[Regulatory-first approach]

OROKAI prioritizes compliance and regulatory clarity before launching features that interact with traditional finance or regulated instruments.

:::
