### Czego OROKAI NIE robi

> <mark>**NIE przechowuje Twoich środków**</mark> – Masz pełną kontrolę nad środkami

> <mark>**NIE ma dostępu do Twoich kluczy**</mark> – Ty jesteś ich właścicielem, zawsze  

> <mark>**NIE wykonuje transakcji za Ciebie**</mark> – Ty podpisujesz każdy krok (Jeden podpis dla wielu kroków)

> <mark>**NIE gwarantuje zwrotów**</mark> – Zwroty są zmienne, pochodzą z zewnętrznych protokołów  

> <mark>**NIE udziela porad inwestycyjnych**</mark> –  Sugestie AI mają charakter wyłącznie informacyjny  

> <mark>**NIE działa jako broker/custodian**</mark> – Jesteśmy oprogramowaniem, nie instytucją finansową

## OROKAI as a Gateway to DeFi

OROKAI is a single platform for initiating and managing positions in verified protocols across <mark>multiple chains</mark> (Ethereum, BNB, Base, Polygon, Solana, Cosmos). 

The interface <mark>simplifies</mark> allocation and potential migration of funds between protocols/networks while maintaining <mark>full self-custody</mark> on the user's side. Rewards generated by selected protocols can be settled in USDT – through user-signed transactions and exchanges on DEX from the list of approved integrations. Returns are variable and not guaranteed.

:::tip[Gateway to DeFi]

OROKAI is a single platform that simplifies allocation and potential migration of funds between protocols/networks while maintaining full self-custody on the user's side.

:::

## OROKAI as an Interface Connecting Web2 and Web3 Worlds

### Software layer, not a financial intermediary

OROKAI is an <mark>interface and a set of smart contracts</mark> facilitating the preparation of on-chain transactions. <mark>We do not control protocols, store funds, or execute transactions on behalf of users.</mark>

### Experiential bridge (UX)

We unify steps familiar from Web2 (transparent screens, confirmations, statuses) with the nature of Web3 (cryptographic signatures, contract permissions).

### Integrations with licensed partners

On/off-ramp and card services are presented in the UI, but settlements and KYC/AML are handled by the partner.

### What happens "behind the scenes" after clicking in the UI

> The interface prepares a transaction bundle (e.g., approve → swap → stake)

> The user verifies costs/risks and signs transactions in their wallet

> Smart contracts execute operations within the granted permissions (<mark>least-privilege principle</mark>)

:::warning[Key principle]

OROKAI serves as an orchestration layer, not a custodian – users retain complete control over their assets at all times.

:::
