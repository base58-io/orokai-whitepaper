# Revenue Sources (Fees)

## Fee Structure

### Card (via partner)

Share in partner's <mark>operational fees and settlements</mark> according to their fee schedule (e.g., issuance/usage fees, selected interchange/rebate components – if agreements provide). OROKAI charges an integration/UI fee where appropriate.

### Staking / rewards / claim-flows

Fees for transaction <mark>preparation and orchestration</mark> (e.g., claim/re-stake, claim→USDT through DEX), presented before signing. Rewards are sourced from external protocols; reward levels are variable and not guaranteed.

### FIAT↔CRYPTO (on-/off-ramp via partner)

Share in partner's revenue (rev-share) or interface fee – according to agreement. Settlements, KYC/AML, PCI DSS remain with the partner.

### DEX swap

Interface fee for route <mark>preparation and cost presentation</mark> (gas/fee/slippage) – without "best execution" claims. Swaps executed by external DEX from allow-list.

### Cross-chain migrations

Fee for flow composition (unstake/withdraw → bridge → swap → deposit) and its UI handling. Bridges/cross-chain communication according to allow-list (e.g., CCTP/Wormhole/LayerZero).

### Network fees (gas) and protocol services on OROKAI chain

Part of the base fee (gas) and service fees (e.g., batching, routing, indexing, cross-chain messages) constitute protocol/infrastructure revenue according to on-chain policy.

:::tip[Transparent fee model]

All fees are disclosed before user authorization – OROKAI monetizes software and infrastructure services, not custody or asset management.

:::
