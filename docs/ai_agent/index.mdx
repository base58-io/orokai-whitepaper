DeFi offers hundreds of protocols across dozens of blockchains, each with different yields, lock-up periods, and risk profiles. 
For someone new to this space, the sheer volume of choices creates a paralyzing problem: 

> How do you know which protocols are safe? 

> Which match your goals? Which are worth the gas fees? 

Most people either spend weeks researching and still feel uncertain, or they follow random advice from social media and hope for the best.

OROKAI's AI Agent works like Google Maps for DeFi. 
Maps shows you the route and possible alternatives, but you decide which route to take and you drive the car.
Similarly, OROKAI AI shows you DeFi strategies and possible alternatives, but you decide which to choose and you sign the transactions.

### Here's how it works
During a quick 5-10 minute onboarding, you answer simple questions about your experience level, goals, risk tolerance, and preferences. 
The AI doesn't store your personal data - instead, it assigns you to generalized context groups that help filter what information you see.
Based on your profile, the AI prepares protocol recommendations and monitors market conditions to suggest adjustments when relevant.

:::warning[The critical difference]

the AI prepares and explains, you decide and sign. Every transaction requires your approval. The AI can't move your money, can't execute trades, and can't access your wallet. 
It's informational only - like Google Maps showing you routes but never driving the car.

:::
