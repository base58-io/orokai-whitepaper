# How It Works (User Journey)

OROKAI <mark>simplifies DeFi entry:</mark> from on-ramp through "zero-setup wallet," strategy selection, first allocation, to monitoring and potential "one-click" migrations between protocols and chains - with full user control (self-custody). 

Crypto exchanges are executed by external DEX from the list of approved integrations; fiat/card settlements are handled by licensed partners. <mark>Protocol returns are variable and not guaranteed;</mark> every operation is signed by the user in their wallet.

## The Complete User Journey

### Stage 1: Entry

**Goal:** Get user from "I want to try DeFi" to "I have funds ready"

**Traditional DeFi Path:**
1. Research wallets (30-60 min)
2. Install wallet, save seed phrase (anxiety: "what if I lose it?")
3. Register on CEX, wait for KYC (1-3 days)
4. Buy crypto on CEX
5. Learn how to withdraw to wallet
6. Pay withdrawal fee + wait for confirmations

**Total time:** 2-5 days | Steps: 15+ | Stress level: High

### Stage 2: Education & Profile

**Goal:** Help user understand options without information overload

**Traditional DeFi Path:**
1. Research protocols (Reddit, YouTube, Discord) - 4-8 hours
2. Try to understand: APY, TVL, audits, risks
3. Comparison paralysis ("Which one is best?")
4. Pick based on incomplete information or FOMO

**Result:** Often suboptimal choice or no choice at all     

#### OROKAI Path:            

> Quick profile setup (5-10 min): Goals, risk, horizon

> AI shows 3 options matched to YOUR Profile

> Clear explanations: "Why this?" reason-codes

> Compare side-by-side (costs, risks, steps)

**Result:** Informed decision in 10-15 minutes 

### Stage 3: First Allocation

**Goal:** Deploy funds into chosen strategy safely

**Traditional DeFi Path:**
1. Go to protocol website (verify URL - phishing risk)
2. Connect wallet (hope it's the real site)
3. Approve contract (often "unlimited" - scary)
4. Wait, sign, pay gas (surprise cost)
5. Stake/deposit (another tx, more gas)
6. Wait for confirmation, check if it worked
7. Realize you need to manually track rewards

**Result:** 5-7 separate transactions | Each with hidden gas cost | 30-60 minutes

#### OROKAI Path:

> Review prepared transaction flow (all steps visible upfront)

> See total costs BEFORE signing anything

> Sign steps in YOUR wallet (we prepare, you authorize)

> Track automatically in unified dashboard

**Result:** Same transactions, but orchestrated & transparent | 10-15 minutes

**Key Difference:** Cost transparency upfront | You still sign everything

### Stage 4: Ongoing Management

**Goal:** Monitor performance, claim rewards, adapt to changes

**Traditional DeFi Path:**
1. Manually check each protocol website for rewards
2. Claim rewards (separate tx + gas each time)
3. Swap rewards to stablecoin (if desired) - more txs
4. Monitor APY changes manually
5. Research alternatives when APY drops
6. Miss opportunities due to time/effort required

**Result:** Suboptimal returns | High overhead (2-4 hours/month)

#### OROKAI Path:

> Unified dashboard shows all positions

> Optional auto-claim + convert to USDT (you authorize once)

> AI monitors APY changes, suggests adjustments

> Notifications for opportunities (low gas, better rates)

> One-click migration flows (you review & sign)

**Result:** Optimized returns | Minimal time (10-20 min/month)

**Key Difference:** Automation ≠ custody | You approve all actions

### Stage 5: Rebalancing / Migration

**Goal:** Move funds to better opportunities efficiently

**Traditional DeFi Path:**
1. Unstake/withdraw from Protocol A (tx + gas)
2. Wait for lock-up period / unstaking queue
3. Bridge to another chain (if needed) - complex, risky
4. Swap asset (if needed) - DEX tx + gas + slippage
5. Approve new protocol (tx + gas)
6. Stake/deposit in Protocol B (tx + gas)

**Result:** 4-6 transactions | High error risk | Hours to days

#### OROKAI Path:

> Compare current vs. alternative (side-by-side)

> Review full migration path (all steps + costs shown upfront)

- All steps clearly listed
- Total migration cost (gas + fees + slippage)
- Break-even analysis: "Migration pays for itself in X days"

> Approve flow, sign steps in YOUR wallet

> Track progress in real-time

**Result:** Same txs, but orchestrated | Lower error risk | 15-30 minutes

**Key Difference:** One-click = one flow, multiple signatures | You control each step

### Stage 6: Exit / Off-Ramp

**Goal:** Convert crypto back to fiat or withdraw

**Traditional DeFi Path:**
1. Unstake/withdraw (wait for lock-up)
2. Swap to major asset (ETH/BTC) if needed
3. Send to CEX (check addresses, networks - error risk)
4. Sell on CEX
5. Withdraw to bank (1-3 days)

**Result:** Multiple steps | High error risk (wrong network = lost funds)

#### OROKAI Path:

> Initiate withdrawal flow

> OROKAI prepares: unstake → swap → off-ramp (via partner)

> Review all steps & costs, sign in YOUR wallet

> Partner handles fiat conversion (KYC on their side)

**Result:** Orchestrated flow | Network verification built-in | Reduced error risk

**Key Difference:** Partner handles fiat, not us | OROKAI = software layer only

:::warning[KEY PRINCIPLES]
> **Self-Custody Throughout**
Your keys, your funds. Always. OROKAI never has access.

> **You Sign Everything**
Every transaction requires your explicit signature. Automation = prepared flows, not execution without permission.

> **Transparent Costs**
All fees (gas, DEX, bridge, OROKAI) shown BEFORE you sign.

> **No Guarantees**
Yields are variable. Protocols are external. We inform, not advise.

> **Blockchain Rules Apply**
Transactions are final (blockchain nature). OROKAI doesn't add or remove this property.

> **Risk Warnings**
Clear labels on complexity, liquidity, protocol risk. YOU decide what level of risk to accept
:::
