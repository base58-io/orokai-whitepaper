# Payment Cards 

## Revenue Sharing Model

### Partner's role

Issuance, settlements, AML/KYC, PCI DSS, fee schedules; partner charges interchange/FX/operational fees according to their own policies.

### OROKAI's role

<mark>Software and integration layer;</mark> no PAN/CVV processing; possible share in partner's revenue (according to agreement) and/or interface fee on our side.

### Example revenue streams (depending on agreement/jurisdiction)

> Share in interchange/rebate (if permitted)

> Share in FX margin (if permitted)

> Operational fees (plastic/virtual, express, chargeback handling – if applicable)

### Utility discount

Token holders/lockers may qualify for discounts on OROKAI fees related to card module (if agreement and jurisdiction permit).

## Transparency

In UI, we communicate who charges which fee (partner vs. OROKAI) before acceptance.

:::warning[Partner-dependent terms]

Card-related fees and revenue sharing depend on partner agreements and local regulations – specific terms may vary by jurisdiction.

:::
