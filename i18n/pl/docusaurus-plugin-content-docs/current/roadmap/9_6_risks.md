# Risks and Mitigations (Summary)

## Risk Categories and Responses

### Technical risks

Contract/bridge errors, MEV, network congestion → audits, allow-list, fair-ordering where possible, operational limits.

### Operational risks

User errors (network, slippage, approve) → warnings, "step-by-step" guidance, revoke functionality.

### Regulatory risks

CEX, RWA, securities, TGE → licensed partners, compliance gates, "utility/not guaranteed" language.

:::warning[Multi-layered risk management]

OROKAI addresses risks at technical, operational, and regulatory levels through proactive controls, clear communication, and strategic partnerships.

:::
