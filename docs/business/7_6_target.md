# Target Customer Segments and Acquisition Channels

## Customer Segments

### New to crypto / outside web3

Need simple entry, USDT settlement, <mark>"step-by-step"</mark> guidance.

### Advanced DeFi users

Value multi-chain, "one-click migrations," metric normalization, signature-based automations.

### B2B/B2B2C (white-label/API)

Fintechs, wallets, aggregators, crypto-native institutions.

## Acquisition Channels

### Affiliate program

Rev-share on fees, attribution tools.

### Partnerships

On-/off-ramp, card issuers, DeFi protocols.

### Content/SEO

<mark>"Prepare & sign" guides,</mark> strategy comparisons with metric normalization.

### Wallet integrations

Deep-links, widgets.

### Product campaigns

New migration/claim "scenarios."

:::tip[Multi-channel approach]

Diverse acquisition channels allow OROKAI to reach both crypto newcomers and experienced users, while B2B partnerships enable institutional-scale adoption.

:::
