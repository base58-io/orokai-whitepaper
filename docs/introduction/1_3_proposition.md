# Unique Value Proposition (USP) and Target Audience

## OROKAI USP

### Non-custodial by design
OROKAI does not hold user funds; it operates as an orchestration layer for transactions signed by the user.

### One place → multiple chains and protocols
DeFi aggregation with automated allocation, claims, and migrations.

### AI Agent
<mark>Informational</mark> recommendations tailored to user profile (preferences, experience, risk tolerance). Does not constitute investment advice.

### "Zero-friction" UX
Maximum simplification of the onboarding process and position management (including on-/off-ramp support through licensed partners).

### Transparency
Rewards generated by protocols may be settled in USDT; their <mark>level is variable and not guaranteed.</mark>


## Target Audience

Individuals from outside web3 (simplicity), crypto users (automation/optimization), B2B/B2B2C partners (API/white-label — non-custodial model)
