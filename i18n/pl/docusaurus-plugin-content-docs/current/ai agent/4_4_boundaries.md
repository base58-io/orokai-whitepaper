# Boundaries and Responsible Use of AI (Disclaimer)

## Scope and Limitations

### No investment advice
Recommendations are <mark>informational in nature.</mark> Decisions and authorizations remain with the user.

### No return/price guarantees
Returns and rates are variable and depend on protocols and the market; <mark>the agent does not guarantee best price or execution.</mark>

### Non-custodial
<mark>Agent does not have access to keys</mark> and does not execute transactions; operates in a prepare-and-explain model.

## Security Controls ("Guardrails")

### Protocol allowlist
Allow-list of protocols/bridges/DEX, exposure limits per protocol and chain.

### Pre-signature verifications
List of steps, costs (gas/slippage/fees), <mark>required smart contract permissions.</mark>

### Anomaly detection
Risk signals (liquidation, liquidity, incidents) → pause and warn.

### Permission revocation
Quick links to revoke/limit approvals.

### Compliance
Geo-restrictions, sanctions, partner rules (on/off-ramp, cards) on their side; we enforce restrictions in UI.

## Transparency and Explainability

### Reason codes
Each proposal has reason codes, list of assumptions, and risk factors.

### Decision log
What, when, and on what basis was suggested – available to users.

### User control
<mark>Users can disable personalization</mark> or reset their profile.

:::warning[Important disclaimer]

**AI Agent OROKAI** provides information and proposals aligned with the user's declared profile. This is not investment advice. All operations require the user's conscious signature in their wallet. Returns are variable and not guaranteed.

:::
