### Common Exit Questions Answered

#### What if I need funds urgently but my position is locked?
- OROKAI shows: Remaining lock-up time, alternative liquid positions
- Suggests: Borrow against position (if supported) or wait X days
- Does NOT: Force early exit at penalty (you decide)

#### What if I want to exit but gas is too high?
- OROKAI shows: Current gas, historical average, your max threshold
- Suggests: Wait for lower gas (set alert) or use lower-cost chain
- Does NOT: Force exit at high cost (you decide timing)

#### What if the protocol I'm using gets hacked?
- OROKAI alerts: Immediate notification if incident detected
- Shows: Emergency withdrawal options, alternative protocols
- Does NOT: Automatically move funds (you stay in control)

#### What if I want to move everything to cold storage?
- OROKAI shows: Unstake all → Consolidate to one chain → Transfer steps
- Provides: Clear addresses, networks, estimated total cost
- Does NOT: Prevent you (full portability guaranteed)
