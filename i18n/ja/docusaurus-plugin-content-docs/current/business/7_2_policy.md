# Fee Policy and Discounts

## Transparent and Flexible Pricing

### General principles

> Fees are disclosed before signing

> No hidden costs – we display components (gas, DEX/bridge/protocol, our interface fee)

### Discount tiers

**Loyalty / volume:** monthly thresholds

**Premium packages:** e.g., extended reporting, pro alerts

**Token utility:** discounts on OROKAI fees and/or access to selected features after voluntary token lock/stake. These are product discounts, not interest or guaranteed benefits.

### Flexibility

<mark>Fee policy may be updated with advance notice</mark> and clear communication in UI.

:::tip[User-aligned incentives]

Discount structures reward engaged users while maintaining transparency – all benefits are clearly defined and disclosed upfront.

:::
