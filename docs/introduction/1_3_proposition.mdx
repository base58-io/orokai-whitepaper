# Unique Value Proposition (USP) and Target Audience

## OROKAI USP

### Non-custodial by design
OROKAI does not hold user funds; it operates as an orchestration layer for transactions signed by the user.

### One interface across multiple chains and protocols
The platform aggregates access to DeFi protocols across different blockchains, simplifying allocation, reward handling, and protocol transitions while preserving user custody.

### Context-aware AI Agent
The Agent provides <mark>informational,</mark> profile-aligned outputs based on user preferences, experience level, and risk alignment. It does not provide investment advice and cannot act on behalf of the user.

### "Zero-friction" UX
Onboarding and position management are designed to minimize operational complexity, with optional guided flows and integrated on- and off-ramps via licensed partners.

### Transparency
Protocol-generated rewards may be settled in reference stable assets such as USDT. <mark>Reward levels are variable, protocol-dependent, and not guaranteed.</mark>

## Target Audience

OROKAI is designed for individuals new to web3 who value simplicity, for crypto-native users seeking aggregation and operational efficiency, and for B2B or B2B2C partners requiring non-custodial infrastructure via APIs or white-label deployments.
