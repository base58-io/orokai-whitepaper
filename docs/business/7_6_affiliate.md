# Affiliate Program

## Program Structure

### Mechanics

Partner-affiliate receives a share in OROKAI's revenue <mark>from fees generated by referred accounts</mark> (e.g., DEX swap, migrations, on-/off-ramp; card components – if agreements permit).

### Attribution

Link/code, UTM parameters, off-/on-chain signatures; attribution window defined in terms and conditions.

### Payouts

In USDT, after meeting thresholds and anti-fraud review; if partner requirements mandate, KYC may be required on their side.

### Anti-fraud

Exclusion rules (self-referrals, sybil, chargeback abuse), payout suspension upon suspicion of abuse, audit trails.

## Compliance

Affiliation concerns fees for interface/integration usage; this is not "interest" or "guaranteed income."

:::warning[Fraud prevention]

OROKAI maintains strict anti-fraud controls to ensure program integrity and compliance with partner agreements and regulatory requirements.

:::
