# Reward Payouts in USDT and Transparent Costs

## Flexible Settlement Options

### USDT settlement (optional)

Rewards generated by selected protocols can be claimed and – if the user configures it – exchanged for USDT through DEX from the allow-list.

### Cost visibility

Before signing a transaction, the UI shows estimated costs (gas, protocol/bridge/DEX fees, slippage).

### No return guarantees

Reward amounts and exchange rates are <mark>variable</mark> and depend on external protocols/market; OROKAI does not guarantee them.

### Reporting

"Results" view normalizes metrics and allows filtering by chain, protocol, strategy, and settlement currency (including USDT).

:::warning[Variable returns]

All returns are subject to market conditions and protocol parameters. USDT conversion happens through DEX at prevailing market rates, which may fluctuate.

:::
