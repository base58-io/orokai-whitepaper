# On-Ramp / Off-Ramp with Credit Card

## Partner-Operated Services

### Crypto purchase/sale

Executed by external, licensed partner (card issuance, settlements, AML/KYC, PCI DSS). <mark>OROKAI provides only software/integration layer</mark> (UI/API/webhooks) and does not process PAN/CVV.

### Funding and withdrawals

Funds come from the user's wallet; fiat↔crypto conversions and card settlements are handled by the partner.

### Cost visibility

Before the user accepts an operation, they see partner fees and network costs (if applicable).

:::tip[Regulated partner integration]

All card and fiat operations are handled by licensed, compliant third-party providers – OROKAI acts solely as the interface layer.

:::
