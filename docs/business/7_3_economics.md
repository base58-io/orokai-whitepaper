# Unit Economics

## Revenue and Cost Structure

### Revenue structure per user (simplified model)

**ARPU ≈** Σ (number of operations × fee per operation) + share in partner revenues (if applicable)

### Main variable costs

Infrastructure costs (RPC/indexers), notification handling, support, partner settlements.

### Main fixed costs

Software development and maintenance, audits, compliance, security.

## Profitability Levers

### 1. Operation volume

Allocations, swaps, migrations vs. infrastructure cost per operation.

### 2. On-chain activity

Gas/services while maintaining throughput and low latency.

### 3. Paid plan conversion

Token-driven discounts (utility) that increase retention/activity.

### 4. Route optimization

Fewer steps = lower churn and higher signature conversion.

### 5. Low-cost acquisition

Affiliation, B2B2C partnerships.

:::tip[Scalability focus]

Unit economics improve with scale – higher volumes drive down per-operation costs while maintaining quality and security standards.

:::
