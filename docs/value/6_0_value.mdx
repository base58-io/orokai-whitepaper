# Value for Users

:::tip[Core Value]

OROKAI reduces DeFi complexity by 80-90% while maintaining 100% user control. You get the benefits of professional-grade DeFi management without giving up custody of your assets.

:::

Minimal effort with controlled risk. OROKAI <mark>simplifies</mark> DeFi onboarding and ongoing position management while <mark>maintaining full user control</mark> (self-custody). Protocol-generated rewards can be settled in USDT, and costs are presented upfront - without the need to learn the nuances of each chain and protocol.

:::warning[Returns]

Returns are variable and not guaranteed; fiat/card settlements are handled by licensed partners; crypto exchanges are DEX-first.

:::

### What you don't give up

> Control
You sign every transaction. No exceptions.
OROKAI cannot move funds, execute trades, or access your wallet.

> Ownership
Your assets stay in YOUR wallet or in protocols YOU choose.
OROKAI never holds, custodies, or controls your funds.

> PRIVACY
Your keys never leave YOUR device.
We don't see seed phrases, private keys, or passwords.

> FLEXIBILITY
Use OROKAI for some positions, direct protocols for others.
Export your wallet anytime (full portability).

> FINAL SAY
Ignore AI suggestions. Dismiss alerts. Cancel flows.
YOU decide everything.

### What it costs

> OROKAI Fees:
- UI/orchestration fee: Applied to specific operations (disclosed upfront)
- On/off-ramp: 1% (+ provider fees)

> Pass-Through Costs (Not OROKAI fees):
- Blockchain gas (paid to validators/miners)

- Yields are variable (set by external protocols)
