# Mission and Vision

## Mission

To build a <mark>secure, understandable, and accessible</mark> bridge to DeFi for users and partners from outside web3 — enabling them to independently benefit from passive asset management opportunities without taking away control over their assets and without the need to master on-chain complexity.

## Vision

An ecosystem where:

> self-custody is the standard (user controls assets),

> UX masks complexity (wallet and gas abstraction, clear permissions),

> compliance-by-design (geo-restrictions, sanctions, exclusions),

> transparency and risk control are product features (limits, whitelisted protocols, warnings).
